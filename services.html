Services

Snagging services

Independent snagging inspections across Munster and Connacht. Every inspection ends with the same thing: a written, photographed snag list you can act on. Prices are quoted per property, so get in touch for a quote.

New Builds

Houses & Apartments

A full snag list inspection of a newly built home before you close the sale. Every room, the attic and the outside of the property are checked, and every defect is photographed and listed for the builder.

  • Full room-by-room inspection
  • Photograph of every snag
  • Written snag list for the builder
Extensions

Extension Snagging

Extensions are usually paid for in stages, with the balance due on completion. An inspection before that final payment records any defects or unfinished work while the builder is still obliged to put them right.

  • Finishes, fittings and joinery checked
  • Windows, doors and roofs inspected
  • Written list for the builder
Rentals

Rental Properties

A snag and condition report for a rental property at handover, recording defects and the general condition room by room before tenants move in, so there is a clear record to refer back to.

  • Room-by-room condition record
  • Photographs throughout
  • Defects listed separately
Found Snags?

What to do when the list comes back

01

Send the list to the builder

Pass the full snag list, with the photographs, to the builder or developer. It is written to be worked from: every item has a location and a photo.

02

Agree a timeframe

Agree when the repairs will be done. Ideally the bulk of the list is cleared before you close; anything outstanding should be agreed in writing with a date.

03

Check the repairs

When the builder says the list is cleared, a re-inspection walks the property against the original snag list and confirms what has been fixed and what has not.
