The Checklist

Room by room, nothing left to memory

A snagging inspection works through a set checklist for every part of the property, so defects are recorded systematically rather than spotted by chance. This is the checklist the inspection follows.

Hall, Stairs & Landing

  • Front door opens, closes and locks smoothly; seals intact
  • Walls and ceilings free of cracks, marks and rough plaster
  • Stair treads even; handrail secure with no movement
  • Flooring level, undamaged and finished at every threshold
  • Light switches, sockets and smoke alarms in place and working

Living Areas

  • Windows open and close properly; glass free of scratches
  • Patio or French doors aligned, locking and sealing correctly
  • Skirting, architraves and coving complete, with no gaps
  • Radiators heating evenly, with no leaks at the valves
  • TV, phone and power points present where expected

Kitchen

  • Unit doors and drawers aligned, undamaged and soft-closing
  • Worktop joints neat; no chips, stains or burn marks
  • Sink taps running with good pressure; no leaks underneath
  • Appliances fitted, level and connected
  • Extractor fan working; tiling and splashback grouted fully

Bathrooms & En-suites

  • Taps, showers and flushes all working, with no drips
  • Sealant complete around baths, showers and basins
  • Tiles fully grouted, with no cracked or hollow tiles
  • Extractor fan running and venting properly
  • Mirrors, rails and accessories fixed securely

Bedrooms

  • Wardrobe doors and drawers aligned and undamaged, where fitted
  • Windows and trickle vents working; glass unmarked
  • Ceilings free of stains that suggest leaks above
  • Sockets and switches tested; aerial points present
  • Flooring finished under doors and at skirting lines

Attic

  • Insulation laid evenly across the full floor area
  • No daylight visible at the roof covering or eaves
  • Pipework and tanks lagged where required
  • Access hatch opening freely and sealing when closed

Heating, Plumbing & Electrics

  • Every socket and light point tested throughout the property
  • Heating system running; each radiator checked room by room
  • Hot and cold water at every outlet, with waste draining freely
  • Stopcock and consumer unit accessible and labelled

Outside & Garden

  • Render and brickwork free of cracks, staining and damage
  • Gutters, downpipes and gullies clear and connected
  • Paths, driveways and patios laid, level and finished
  • Fences, gates and boundaries complete and secure
  • Outside tap and lights fitted and working, where supplied
